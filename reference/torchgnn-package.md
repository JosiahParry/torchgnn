# torchgnn: Graph Neural Networks for 'torch'

Build and train graph neural networks on top of the 'torch' deep
learning framework. Message-passing layers are provided for the Graph
Convolutional Network (GCN) of Kipf and Welling (2017)
[doi:10.48550/arXiv.1609.02907](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1609.02907)
, the GraphSAGE architecture of Hamilton, Ying and Leskovec (2017)
[doi:10.48550/arXiv.1706.02216](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1706.02216)
, the Graph Attention Network (GAT) of Velickovic and others (2018)
[doi:10.48550/arXiv.1710.10903](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1710.10903)
, the Graph Isomorphism Network (GIN) of Xu and others (2019)
[doi:10.48550/arXiv.1810.00826](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1810.00826)
, and the regional GCN of Guo and others (2025)
[doi:10.1080/24694452.2025.2558661](https://doi.org/10.1080/24694452.2025.2558661)
. These layers are composed into ready-made node-level and graph-level
models. Supporting utilities construct sparse adjacency matrices from
edge lists, normalize them, pool node embeddings into graph-level
representations, and split graphs into training, validation and test
sets.
